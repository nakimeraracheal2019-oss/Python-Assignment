📌 What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity involves protecting computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, and damage. In our increasingly digital world, cybersecurity has become more important than ever.

⚠️ Common Cyber Attacks

Phishing, malware, ransomware, and social engineering are some of the most common cyber threats today. Understanding these threats is the first step in protecting yourself.

🧪 Digital Forensics Investigation

Digital forensics involves collecting, preserving, and analyzing digital evidence to investigate cybercrimes. Key steps include:

  • Evidence Collection
  • Data Analysis
  • Timeline Reconstruction
  • Reporting Findings

🛡️ How to Stay Safe

Always use strong passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and avoid suspicious links or downloads. Stay informed about the latest threats and follow best practices for cybersecurity.
